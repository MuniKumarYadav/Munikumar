What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO targets traditional organic search, AEO targets direct-answer experiences, and GEO focuses on making content clear, attributable and useful in generative search. In practice, a strong content system can use all three.

How should I start learning digital marketing?

Start with audience intent and search fundamentals, then build skills in technical SEO, content strategy, analytics, AEO and GEO. Move from concepts to implementation by applying each topic to a real page or campaign.

How can I make content easier for search and AI systems to understand?

Use clear headings, concise answers, structured lists or tables, descriptive internal links, explicit entities, credible sources and visible author/update information. These structures also make pages easier for people to scan.
