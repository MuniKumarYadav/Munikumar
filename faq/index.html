Answers

Digital marketing questions, answered directly.

A concise reference for businesses deciding how SEO, AEO, GEO, paid acquisition, content and analytics fit into a growth strategy.

What is SEO?

SEO is the practice of improving a website so search engines can discover, understand and surface its useful content for relevant searches.

What is AEO?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is an answer-oriented content practice that structures information so important questions are answered clearly and directly.

What is GEO?

GEO, used here as generative engine optimization, is a working approach to making an organization's entity, expertise, evidence and content easier for generative systems to understand and potentially cite.

Who is digital marketing best for?

Digital marketing can support businesses that need awareness, qualified traffic, leads, sales or retention and have a measurable customer journey.

How long does SEO take?

SEO timing varies by competition, site condition, authority, content quality and search demand. It should be treated as a compounding channel rather than a fixed-duration campaign.

What should a business measure?

Choose metrics that connect activity to outcomes: qualified traffic, conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, revenue or pipeline, retention and channel efficiency.
