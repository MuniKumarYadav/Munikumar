03 / SEARCH EVOLUTION

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

Answer: SEO focuses on organic discoverability, AEO focuses on clear answers to specific questions, and GEO focuses on making information understandable and useful for generative search systems. They can reinforce one another.

ApproachPrimary purposeUseful when
SEOOrganic discoverabilityCustomers need to find your pages through search.
AEODirect answerabilityCustomers ask specific questions and need concise responses.
GEOGenerative visibilityAI search needs clear entities, evidence and context.
04 / PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

What should a business check before scaling digital marketing?

Use this checklist to connect content and search work with the business problem instead of publishing for volume alone.

  • Audience and intent: identify who the page serves, what they need and what decision follows.
  • Discoverability: confirm important pages are crawlable, relevant and internally linked.
  • Answerability: place concise answers, definitions and supporting context near important questions.
  • Entity clarity: keep the organization, services, authorship and evidence consistent across the site.
  • Conversion: connect useful visibility to a clear next step such as a service page, resource or project conversation.
  • Measurement: review qualified engagement and business outcomes before expanding the workflow.

06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who should use SEO, AEO and GEO services?

Business owners, local brands, service companies and growing organizations can use these approaches when online visibility, qualified leads, trust and digital information quality affect business decisions.

How does AI automation support digital marketing?

AI automation can reduce repetitive work in research, content operations, lead workflows and reporting while keeping strategy, quality control and measurement connected to business outcomes.

What should I improve first?

Start with the business objective and customer journey, then address the most important visibility or workflow constraint. Build the minimum useful system, measure it and improve it before adding complexity.
