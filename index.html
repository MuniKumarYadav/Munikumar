FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI-powered digital marketing?

AI-powered digital marketing uses artificial intelligence to support research, ideation, content production, analysis and workflow efficiency while people remain responsible for strategy and decisions.

Who is this website and CRM for?

It is designed for founders, small businesses, marketing teams and independent marketers who need a practical way to organize leads, clients, campaigns and follow-up work.

How does the CRM help a digital marketer?

It gives an authenticated user one workspace to store and review clients, leads, campaigns and open tasks.

Should I use AI for every marketing task?

No. AI is most useful when it supports a defined workflow. Strategy, brand judgment, accuracy and final decisions still require human review.
