07 / Questions

Answers to common growth questions.

Short answers first. Read the full FAQ or our insights for more.

What does Munikumar do?

Munikumar is a digital marketing growth studio providing SEO, paid acquisition, social and content, conversion strategy, analytics and growth strategy.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps search engines discover and understand useful pages. AEO emphasizes direct answers to important questions. GEO makes your organization's entity, expertise, evidence and content easier for generative AI systems to understand and cite.

Who is this digital marketing service for?

Businesses and teams that need stronger search visibility, qualified demand, better conversion or clearer marketing measurement.

How should a business choose a marketing channel?

Choose based on the commercial constraint: SEO for durable organic demand, paid acquisition for controlled testing and immediate reach, social and content for attention and trust, and conversion strategy when traffic isn't turning into enough enquiries or sales.

How is marketing performance measured?

Using business-relevant signals such as qualified traffic, conversion rate, acquisition cost, revenue or pipeline, retention and channel efficiency.
