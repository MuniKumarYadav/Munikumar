01 / services

How can SEO, AEO and GEO work together?

They work as layers rather than replacements. SEO creates strong crawlable and discoverable content, AEO makes important answers easy to extract, and GEO improves entity clarity, evidence and context for generative experiences.

SEO

Technical SEO, topical authority, internal linking, content briefs and structured data.

AEO

Question-led content, direct answers, definitions, lists and useful decision support.

GEO

Generative-search readiness through entities, evidence, brand context and credible citations.

AI Content

Human-led, AI-assisted research and publishing focused on usefulness, originality and conversion.

02 / the evolution

What is the evolution from SEO to AEO to GEO?

Search is expanding from classic ranking pages toward direct answers and generative recommendations. The practical response is to make content useful, structured, attributable and evidence-led.

01 — SEO

Be found

Match search intent with technically strong pages, useful content, internal links, authority and clear site architecture.

02 — AEO

Be the answer

Answer natural-language questions directly. Use definitions, steps, tables and concise summaries where they genuinely help.

03 — GEO

Be understood

Strengthen entities, evidence, first-hand experience and third-party context so generative systems have clearer signals about your brand.

03 / practical framework

How to improve AI-search visibility

Use this checklist when planning a page, service, product or editorial article.

AEO checklist

  • ✓ Put a concise answer immediately after important questions.
  • ✓ Add definitions in plain language.
  • ✓ Use numbered steps, bullets and comparison tables where useful.
  • ✓ Cover conversational questions such as “what is”, “how to” and “should I”.
  • ✓ Keep claims specific and support important facts with credible sources.

GEO checklist

  • ✓ Clearly identify the organization, author, product or service.
  • ✓ Demonstrate first-hand experience, examples or original process evidence.
  • ✓ Connect related entities consistently across the site.
  • ✓ Earn relevant third-party mentions and citations.
  • ✓ Keep important pages current and publish meaningful updates.

05 / evidence & experience

What does a practical GEO workflow look like?

This is an implementation framework, not a claim of guaranteed AI citations. Test each layer against your own audience, queries and business data.

INPUT

Collect evidence

Customer questions, product facts, first-hand observations, case-study material, original research and credible references.

BUILD

Make entities clear

Use consistent names, authorship, organization information, related concepts and structured data across important pages.

MEASURE

Test discovery

Track search visibility, qualified traffic, brand mentions, referral quality and AI-answer observations without assuming every impression is attributable.

06 / frequently asked questions

SEO, AEO & GEO questions

What is SEO?

SEO is the practice of improving a website so search engines can crawl, understand and surface useful pages for relevant searches. It includes technical quality, information architecture, content, links and user-focused relevance.

What is AEO?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on making important answers clear, concise, well-structured and easy to extract in answer-oriented search experiences.

What is GEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. In this site, it refers to improving the clarity, entities, evidence, experience and context that can help a brand be represented accurately in generative search.

Should I choose SEO, AEO or GEO?

For most businesses, use them as layers. Start with SEO fundamentals, add question-and-answer structures where users need direct answers, then strengthen entity and evidence signals for generative discovery.
