05 / Search strategy

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

Each discipline solves a different discovery problem, so the strategy should match the way your audience searches for information.

SEO improves visibility in traditional search results. AEO structures useful information so answer engines can provide direct responses. GEO focuses on making an organization’s entity, expertise, evidence and content easier for generative AI systems to understand and represent.

SEOSearch rankings, crawlability, topical relevance
AEOQuestions, direct answers, structured information
GEOEntities, context, evidence, AI discoverability

06 / Decision support

When should a business use SEO, AEO or GEO?

Choose the approach according to the discovery problem you need to solve: rankings, direct answers, AI visibility, or a combination of all three.

  • Use SEO when you need sustainable organic search visibility and qualified search traffic.
  • Use AEO when customers ask specific questions and you want your content to provide concise, extractable answers.
  • Use GEO when your priority is improving how AI systems understand your brand, expertise and supporting evidence.
  • Combine them when your audience discovers and evaluates your business through both search engines and AI assistants.

07 / FAQ

What do businesses commonly ask about SEO, AEO and GEO?

Each answer starts with the direct takeaway so people and answer engines can understand the key point quickly.

What does Muni Kumar do?

Answer: Muni Kumar helps businesses and personal brands improve organic search visibility, AI-search discoverability and digital conversion through SEO, AEO, GEO, content, paid media and analytics.

What is AEO and why does it matter?

Answer: Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures content around real user questions and concise answers so search engines and AI answer systems can understand and surface useful information more easily.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

Answer: SEO focuses on traditional search visibility, AEO focuses on direct answers to user questions, and GEO focuses on making entities, expertise, evidence and content easier for generative AI systems to understand and represent.

Who is this digital marketing service for?

Answer: It is designed for businesses, startups, SaaS teams, local businesses and personal brands that need stronger search visibility, qualified demand, better conversion or clearer marketing measurement.

How can a business optimize content for AI search?

Answer: Start with real questions, provide concise answers immediately, organize related topics clearly, demonstrate first-hand expertise, support factual claims with credible sources, and use consistent entity information and structured data.

How should a business choose between SEO, AEO and GEO?

Answer: Use SEO for durable organic search visibility, AEO for question-led answer opportunities, GEO for generative AI discoverability, and combine all three when customers use multiple discovery paths.
