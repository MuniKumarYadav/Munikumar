How to choose a digital marketing tool

  1. Define the marketing job: visibility, acquisition, conversion, retention or measurement.
  2. Choose a category and shortlist tools that match the workflow.
  3. Check the official vendor site for current pricing, integrations, limits and data policies.
  4. Test the smallest useful workflow before committing to a large stack.
  5. Measure business outcomes rather than collecting tools for their own sake.
