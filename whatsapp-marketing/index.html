Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is this connected to the official WhatsApp API?

The service is designed around Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform Cloud API—not unofficial WhatsApp Web automation.

Can I message any phone number in a list?

No. You must have the recipient's valid opt-in for the relevant message type, follow applicable laws and honor opt-outs. Purchased or scraped lists should not be used.

Do I need approved templates?

Business-initiated conversations generally require approved message templates. Free-form replies are available within the customer service window under Meta's rules.

Are WhatsApp message fees included?

Meta pricing and any applicable service charges depend on the recipient market, message category and current platform terms. They should be confirmed before launch.
