What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on helping search engines discover, understand and rank useful pages. AEO emphasizes concise answers to explicit questions and answer-oriented retrieval. GEO is a broader working approach for making a brand, entity and evidence easier for generative systems to understand and cite. These practices overlap; they are not three isolated channels.
